# Step 6 — Talent Pool

> "This is your sacred gold mine. I'm double tapping all these people every 3 months; my goal is to get them into the interviews sooner or later."
> "At the start of Q4, I just double tapped everyone → 25 interviews booked. I believe we'll hire 5-7 soon."

---

## What the talent pool is

The talent pool is a curated list of profiles that:
- Passed your AI evaluation (Tier A or B)
- Passed your human review (Talent Review = Yes)
- Were NOT hired yet (either not reached out to, or replied but timing was wrong)

It is NOT a dump of everyone you ever sourced. It is a vetted shortlist of people you genuinely believe would be strong hires when the time is right.

---

## How Bence runs his talent pool

### The "double tap" cadence
- Every 3 months, re-contact everyone in the talent pool
- Simple re-engagement message: "Hey, timing might be better now — still interested in chatting?"
- At Q4 start: reached out to entire pool → 25 interviews booked → expects 5-7 hires

### Why this works
1. Timing matters more than interest. Many people who said "not now" in Q2 are open in Q4.
2. The second touch shows persistence without being pushy. It signals the role is still live and you remember them.
3. Your pool has already been evaluated — no wasted cycles on unqualified people.

## How to structure your talent pool

### Separate tab in your sourcing sheet OR a dedicated CRM view in Gem

Minimum columns:
```
A: Full Name
B: LinkedIn URL
C: Portfolio / GitHub URL  
D: Role type they match (Backend / Design / PM / etc.)
E: Seniority level
F: Current company
G: Location
H: Why they are in the pool (one sentence)
I: Date added to pool
J: Last contacted
K: Last reply / outcome
L: Next touch date
M: Notes (anything personal — kids, side project, location preference)
```

### Tagging system
Tag every profile with:
- **Role type:** Engineering, Design, Product, GTM
- **Seniority:** Junior, Mid, Senior, Staff, Principal
- **Status:** Never contacted / Contacted-no reply / Replied-not now / Replied-interested / In process
- **Priority:** Hire now if possible / Hire next quarter / Long-term pipeline

---

## The quarterly re-engagement cadence

### Month 1 of each quarter
- Review the pool
- Remove anyone who has been hired elsewhere (check LinkedIn)
- Remove anyone who has become clearly irrelevant (big career pivot, moved to different city)
- Add new profiles from the current quarter's sourcing runs

### Month 2 of each quarter
- Double tap everyone tagged "Replied-not now" or "Never contacted"
- Personalise each message (reference what has changed: new funding, new team, new project)

### Month 3 of each quarter
- Close the loop on anyone in active process
- Archive anyone who has explicitly declined or is clearly not open
- Plan the next quarter's sourcing focus based on hiring plan

## Re-engagement message templates

### First re-engagement (previously replied "not now")
```
Hi [Name],

We spoke [X months] ago about [role] at [company]. Timing wasn't right then — 
I'm reaching back out because [specific reason: new project/funding/team/direction].

Still open to a quick catch-up?

[Your name]
```

### Second re-engagement (never replied to first outreach)
```
Hi [Name],

I reached out a few months back about [role] at [company]. In case my message 
got lost: [one sentence on what makes this role interesting specific to their background].

Worth 15 minutes?

[Your name]
```

### Q4 / Q1 refresh message (mass re-engagement)
```
Hi [Name],

New quarter, new energy. I wanted to reconnect — [company] has [specific update: 
launched X / raised Y / grown to Z people / opened new office].

The [role] is still open and I still think your background in [specific signal 
from their profile] is exactly what we need.

Still open to a chat?

[Your name]
```

## The self-reinforcing flywheel from the pool

Every quarter, use your talent pool to improve your sourcing:

```
Step 1: Filter pool to Tier A profiles (your best-quality finds)
Step 2: Run look-alike search prompt using these profiles as input
Step 3: New profiles discovered → run through full flywheel
Step 4: Best new profiles → added to pool
Step 5: Pool quality improves every quarter
```

The longer you run this, the better your pool gets. After 6 months, you should be able to fill 
a new role within 2 weeks from pool re-engagement alone — before any new sourcing is needed.
