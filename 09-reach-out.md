## Bence's honest take on outreach

Bence is direct about this: he does NOT use LLMs to generate personalised outreach at ElevenLabs.
His reason (implied): with a highly curated pool and strong signal filtering, the outreach 
is simpler and more human — because the hard work was done in steps 1-6.

He DOES use ElevenLabs' own voice AI agents to pitch roles — which makes sense for a voice AI 
company to dogfood their own product.

**The lesson:** The goal of the flywheel is to produce a small, high-quality shortlist where 
each person genuinely belongs there. When you have 20 strong profiles instead of 200 weak ones, 
personalisation becomes easier because you are reaching out to fewer, better-matched people.

## The three outreach channels

### 1. LinkedIn Recruiter (InMail)
Best for: Most professional profiles
Response rate baseline: 10–15% (generic), 25–40% (specific)
Key rule: Reference something specific from their profile in the first sentence

### 2. Email sequencing
Best for: When you have verified email from Clay/Apollo/Hunter.io
Tools: Instantly, Apollo, Outreach, or simple Gmail + a scheduler
Response rate baseline: 15–25% for cold email with strong personalisation

### 3. Warm introductions
Best for: Hardest-to-reach candidates (Staff+, Principal, known experts)
How to find: Ask your engineering team "do you know anyone at [target company]?"
Response rate: 60–80% (dramatically higher — the intro removes the cold barrier)

## Message structure (works across all channels)

```
Line 1: [Specific reference to something they built/wrote/contributed to]
Line 2: [One sentence on the problem we're solving — technical, not corporate]
Line 3: [Why their specific background is relevant — one phrase]
Line 4: [Low-commitment ask]
```

**Under 80 words total. Every time.**

## Message examples by role type

### Engineering — Backend (Go contributor)
```
Hi [Name],

Saw your contributions to [specific repo] — particularly [specific PR or feature].

We're building [technical problem in one sentence] at [company]. 

Your [specific skill from their profile] maps directly to what we're working on.

Worth 15 minutes to see if it's interesting?

[Your name]
```

### Design (Dribbble / portfolio signal)
```

We're building [product description] and the design challenge is [specific].

Given your background in [specific category from classifier], I think you'd 
find the problem interesting.

Open to a quick chat?

[Your name]

### Product (Substack / writing signal)
```
Hi [Name],

```

and we're building the [GTM motion] from scratch.

The motion is the same; the market is different. Worth a call?

[Your name]
```

## Sequence structure (for email)

```
Day 1: First outreach (message above)
Day 6: Follow-up if no reply
  → "Hi [Name] — following up on my note from last week about [role] at [company]. 
     Happy to share more context if useful."
Day 14: Final touch if no reply
  → "I'll leave you alone after this, but wanted to share one more thing: 
     [one new piece of context — new project, new funding, specific team info].
     If timing is ever right, reach out."
```

**Three touches maximum. Never a fourth message.**

## The warm intro process

When you have a high-value target who is unlikely to respond to cold outreach:

1. Add the profile to a "Warm Intro Needed" tag in your sheet
2. Share with your hiring manager: "Do you know anyone who could introduce us to [Name]?"
3. Share with the engineering team lead: "Does anyone on the team know [Name] or know someone who does?"
4. Check mutual LinkedIn connections — ask your recruiter network
5. If you find an intro path: brief the introducer in 2 sentences on why this person and what you are looking for
6. Let the intro happen naturally — do not write the message for them

**The golden rule:** A warm intro is worth 10 cold InMails. Spend 30 minutes finding an intro path before sending cold outreach to a high-value target.

## Tracking outreach in your sheet

Update these columns after every outreach action:

| Column | What to record |
|---|---|
| Outreach Date | Date first message sent |
| Last Touch Date | Date of most recent contact |
| Last Reply / Outcome | What they said, or "No reply" |
| Next Action | "Follow up [date]" or "In process" or "Closed" |
| Status | Update to current stage |
